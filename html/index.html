FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do I need experience to join? +
Absolutely not. We welcome everyone — from complete beginners to seasoned athletes. Our trainers tailor programs to your current fitness level and goals.
Can I try before I commit? +
Yes! We offer a free 3-day trial pass so you can experience the gym, meet the trainers, and attend classes before choosing a membership plan.
What are your opening hours? +
We're open Monday–Friday 5AM–12AM, Saturday 6AM–10PM, and Sunday 7AM–9PM. Elite and Champion members also have 24/7 keycard access.
Is there parking available? +
Yes, we have a large free parking lot directly in front of the gym with over 100 spaces available at all times.
Do you offer personal training? +
Yes. All our trainers are certified and available for 1-on-1 sessions. Personal training is included in the Champion plan or available as an add-on for other plans.
Can I cancel my membership anytime? +
Yes. All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel with 30 days notice from within your account or at the front desk.
